About me

I started in web development through a Diploma in Computer Science and Engineering, and the work followed from there: six months as a web development intern at Rashmitha Information System, where I built the Mini Supermarket application using C#, .NET, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap. I am now completing a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at the same institute.

The AI side of my work is still foundational: the ideas from my degree applied to real problems, not production models I have run for years. Saying so plainly is better than dressing it up. What I can offer today is someone who learns a stack quickly, builds carefully, and states the limits of what has been tried so far.

I am after freelance and project-based work; internship placements are not the goal. If you have something that needs building or fixing, I would like to hear about it.
